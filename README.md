## Featured Projects

### Visual Analysis of Water Meter Anomalies with Vision-Language Models

*Flagship — Final-Year Internship Project at SRM-CS*

Faulty water meters are normally diagnosed by manual visual inspection — slow, inconsistent, and hard to scale. I prepared a multimodal dataset of 445 images (92 real healthy images and 353 defective examples, synthetically generated with Gemini from the healthy images and then manually validated), built a structured multiple-choice (MCQ) evaluation, and benchmarked 12 Vision-Language Models on detection, classification, localization and description. Methodology only is shown — no confidential SRM-CS data is published.

`Python` `Transformers` `Gemini` `Vertex AI` `Pandas` `VLM Evaluation`

🔗 [github.com/HajarBoutayeb/WATER_METER_AI_PFE](https://github.com/HajarBoutayeb/WATER_METER_AI_PFE)

### Autonomous Multi-Modal Invoice Agent

*AI Agents · LangGraph*

Invoice processing mixes PDFs, scanned images and business rules. A stateful LangGraph workflow: Docling parses PDFs, Groq Llama 3.1 handles text, Gemini 1.5 Flash handles vision, Pydantic enforces structured outputs, and conditional rules (amount thresholds, VIP tiers) route each invoice. Results persist to SQLite.

`LangGraph` `Docling` `Gemini` `Groq` `Pydantic` `SQLite`

🔗 [github.com/HajarBoutayeb/autonomous-invoice-agent](https://github.com/HajarBoutayeb/autonomous-invoice-agent)

### RAG Chatbot — Tableau Course Assistant

*RAG · LLMs · LangChain*

A LangChain RAG pipeline over Tableau course material with MultiQuery Retrieval, reranking, and RAGAS evaluation of answer quality — powered by Groq Llama 3.3 70B.

`LangChain` `RAG` `RAGAS` `Groq Llama 3.3 70B` `Reranking`

🔗 [github.com/HajarBoutayeb/langchain-rag-tableau-assistant](https://github.com/HajarBoutayeb/langchain-rag-tableau-assistant)

### Global Health — Decision Dashboards

*Data Analytics · Tableau*

A 134 MB global health dataset (22 variables) is unusable for decisions in raw form. Cleaning with median imputation and deduplication, engineered features (Disease Burden Index from DALYs, Risk Level), full EDA, and three Tableau dashboards covering global and geographical disease analysis.

`Tableau` `Python` `Pandas` `EDA` `Feature Engineering`

🔗 [github.com/HajarBoutayeb/Data-Analytics-Decision-Dashboards](https://github.com/HajarBoutayeb/Data-Analytics-Decision-Dashboards)
