About

Data science, built on evaluation — not demos.

I hold a Master's in Data Science & Big Data (Data Science track) from FSBM, Casablanca. My final-year internship at SRM-CS focused on a question that matters in the field: can Vision-Language Models reliably diagnose anomalies on real water meters?

To answer it properly, I prepared and validated a multimodal dataset of 445 images — 92 real healthy meter images and 353 defective examples, synthetically generated with Gemini from those healthy images and then manually validated — built a structured multiple-choice (MCQ) evaluation, and compared 12 VLMs on detection, classification, localization and description.

Alongside computer vision, I build LLM systems that do real work: a LangGraph multi-modal invoice agent and a LangChain RAG assistant, both with structured outputs and evaluation. I also do the analytics side well — SQL queries and Python data processing, then Tableau and Power BI dashboards grounded in careful data cleaning.

My objective: a Junior Data Scientist, Data Analyst or AI Engineer role in Casablanca, Morocco, where evaluation and data quality matter as much as the model.

Autonomous Multi-Modal Invoice Agent

Problem: Invoice processing mixes PDFs, scanned images and business rules — manual entry is slow and error-prone.

Approach: A stateful LangGraph workflow: Docling parses PDFs, Groq Llama 3.1 handles text, Gemini 1.5 Flash handles vision, Pydantic enforces structured outputs, and conditional rules (amount thresholds, VIP tiers) route each invoice. Results persist to SQLite.
